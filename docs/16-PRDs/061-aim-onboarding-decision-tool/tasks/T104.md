## Steps

1. Create `logic/recommendTier.ts` as a pure TypeScript module with no Vue or store imports.
2. Implement `recommendTier(wizardState)` returning the recommended tier. Take thresholds and rules from the prototype and design-consolidated.md; do not invent values.
3. Apply the prototype bug-fixes: budget is read from monthly or annual, with annual divided by 12 before comparison, and the history qualification uses the 12-month threshold.

5. Keep the function deterministic so T107 can unit-test it.

- [ ] An annual budget of N gives the same result as a monthly budget of N/12.

- [ ] The effective tier is the override when present, otherwise the recommended tier.
